# Coaching fees

Language for the institute's fees, parent portal, and dropout follow-up.

## Language

**Centre**:
A physical branch of the coaching institute.
_Avoid_: Campus, tenant

**Batch**:
A named group of students studying together, such as JEE 2027 Morning.
_Avoid_: Class, course

**Fee plan**:
A student's agreed fees and schedule of instalments, including applicable discounts.
_Avoid_: Subscription, invoice plan

**Instalment**:
A portion of a student's fees with its own due date and outstanding amount.
_Avoid_: EMI, installment

**Fee ledger**:
The record of a student's agreed fees, discounts, instalments, payments, and remaining balance.
_Avoid_: Wallet, account balance

**Outstanding amount**:
The unpaid portion of an agreed charge, whether or not its due date has passed.
_Avoid_: Overdue amount, collected amount

**Overdue amount**:
An outstanding amount whose due date has passed.
_Avoid_: Total outstanding, future dues

**Earned fees**:
The portion of a student's discounted course fees attributable to the calendar days of the course already used, including the withdrawal day.
_Avoid_: Money collected, paid fees

**Unearned fees**:
The portion of a student's discounted course fees attributable to unused course days.
_Avoid_: Refund, unpaid fees

**Refund**:
Money returned from a student's collected fees when those collections exceed earned fees, after accounting for earlier refunds.
_Avoid_: Discount, cancellation of unpaid dues

**Withdrawal**:
A student's departure from a course on a recorded date, ending future course participation and triggering the refund policy.
_Avoid_: Deletion, dropout-risk flag

**Unmatched payment**:
Money received that cannot yet be assigned to an instalment under the payment rules.
_Avoid_: Paid instalment, failed payment

**Payment allocation**:
The portion of received money explicitly assigned to a student's instalment.
_Avoid_: Receipt, refund

**Payment reversal**:
A recorded correction that offsets an earlier financial entry while preserving the original entry.
_Avoid_: Edited receipt, deleted payment

**Refund payout**:
The recorded return of money that settles some or all of an approved refund.
_Avoid_: Refund calculation, withdrawal confirmation

**Net collections**:
Allocated payments received during a reporting period, less payment reversals and refund payouts recorded during that period.
_Avoid_: Billed fees, outstanding amount

**Parent**:
A student's parent or guardian who can view that student's dues and make payments.
_Avoid_: Customer, student login

**Accounts executive**:
An institute staff member who manages student fees and collection exceptions.
_Avoid_: Superadmin, accountant

**Dropout-risk flag**:
An indication that a student's missed instalments, attendance, or test trends warrant a human follow-up.
_Avoid_: Dropout prediction, automatic fee action

**Counsellor call task**:
An internal request for a staff member to review a risk flag and consider contacting the family.
_Avoid_: Parent notification, automatic reminder
