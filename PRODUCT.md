# Setu coaching fees

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, and shadcn/ui, chosen by the user. Deploy the personal demo to Vercel Hobby and Neon Free. Use frontend-design and shadcn skills for the interface.

## Users

Parents use low-end phones to understand and pay their children's dues without calling the institute. Accounts executives work by centre and batch. One central executive can see all four centres.

## Product purpose

Track coaching fees, payments, reminders, and refunds in one consistent record. Give parents the amount, due date, and payment action immediately. Help staff resolve unpaid and unmatched amounts, publish academic updates, and review dropout-risk flags.

## Operating context

A fictional demo of the supplied Jaipur coaching brief. Four centres, eight batches, and 80 synthetic students include the three supplied example rows. The complete assignment and accepted rules are recorded in docs/assignment.md and docs/design.md.

## Capabilities and constraints

Deliver every assignment must-have. Keep payments, OTP delivery, messages, refunds, and external-system connections as clearly labelled demo adapters backed by database records. Isolate each reviewer's data. AI creates internal review tasks only and uses synthetic anonymous summaries through a free OpenRouter model.

## Brand commitments

Original minimal design, restrained palette, clear type, and purposeful screens. Setu is the fictional demo identity. Hindi and English are required for the parent portal and reminders.

## Product principles

- Derive money and dashboard figures from the same financial history.
- Keep the parent's next action immediately clear.
- Make exceptions visible and recoverable.
- Preserve posted history and show uncertainty honestly.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Support narrow phone layouts, readable Hindi, keyboard operation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. Use explicit labels and actionable error messages.
